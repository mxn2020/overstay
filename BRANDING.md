# 🎨 Overstay — Brand Guidelines

## Name

**Overstay** — clean, one-word, instantly understood. It's both a verb and a lifestyle.

**Alternatives considered:** LastOne, 2:43AM, NotLeaving, The Oracle, Chaos Clock

---

## Tagline

> **"We know you're not leaving."**

**Secondary taglines:**
- "The nightlife oracle."
- "Your night, predicted."
- "One last drink. Sure."

---

## Tone of Voice

### The Personality
Overstay speaks like your **funniest, most savage friend** — the one who calls you out but you can't be mad at. Think: roast comedy meets astrology memes.

### Tone Principles

| Principle     | Do                                    | Don't                              |
|---------------|---------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| **Funny**     | Witty one-liners, absurd predictions  | Slapstick, try-hard humor          |
| **Savage**    | Playful roasts, accurate call-outs    | Mean-spirited, personal attacks    |
| **Relatable** | Universal nightlife truths            | Niche references, inside jokes     |
| **Safe**      | "Time distortion oracle"             | "Drink more," promote binge        |
| **Confident** | Bold claims, no hedging              | "Maybe," "possibly," "might"       |

### Example Copy

**Loading screens:**
- "Analyzing your poor decisions…"
- "Calculating regret probability…"
- "Consulting the chaos oracle…"
- "Detecting vibes… and lies…"

**Predictions:**
- "You said 'last one' at 9:15 PM. That was a lie."
- "Your Uber home will cost more than your dinner."
- "Someone in this photo is waking up at 2 PM tomorrow."

**Beat the AI:**
- Win: "Wow, self-control. How boring."
- Lose: "Called it. You're predictable. And we respect that."

## Logo Concept

- Clock showing an absurd late time (e.g., 2:47)
- Bold, geometric sans-serif wordmark
- Works in both full color and single-color
- Minimum size: 24px height

---

## Responsible Messaging

### Always:
- Frame as entertainment / humor
- Use terms like "nightlife oracle," "vibe predictor," "time distortion"
- Include "drink responsibly" in footer

### Never:
- Encourage excessive drinking
- Mock substance abuse
- Use language that normalizes dangerous behavior
- Feature visibly intoxicated individuals negatively
