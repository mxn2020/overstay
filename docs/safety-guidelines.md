# 🛡️ Overstay — Safety & Responsible Messaging Guidelines

## Core Principle

Overstay is an **entertainment app about time perception and nightlife humor**. It is NOT a drinking app, alcohol promotion tool, or encouragement to drink more.

---

## Positioning

### We Are:
- A "nightlife oracle" / "vibe predictor" / "time distortion calculator"
- Entertainment based on universal social behavior
- Humor about the gap between "what we say" and "what we do"

### We Are NOT:
- A drinking game
- An alcohol tracker
- A promoter of binge drinking
- A tool that encourages staying out later

---

## Content Rules

### Language — DO:
- ✅ "How long until you leave" (neutral)
- ✅ "Your night predicted" (fun)
- ✅ "Chaos rating" (playful)
- ✅ "Overstay duration" (the universal lie)
- ✅ "Vibe analysis" (framing = analysis)

### Language — DON'T:
- ❌ "How drunk will you get" (promoting)
- ❌ "Keep drinking" (encouragement)
- ❌ "You need more shots" (dangerous)
- ❌ "Lightweight" or shaming sobriety (toxic)
- ❌ "Alcohol endurance" (glorifying)

---

## AI Prediction Safety

### Non-Alcoholic Scenarios
If the AI detects no alcohol in the image, predictions should still work:
- "You're at a venue. Time distortion applies regardless of what's in your glass."
- Never penalize or mock users for not drinking

### De-Escalation Signals
If the AI detects concerning signals:
- Someone visibly unwell → Skip prediction, show: "Hey, check on your friend. That's more important than a prediction."
- Extremely late hours + high chaos → Subtly: "Getting home safe is the real W tonight."

### Mandatory Footer
Every prediction card includes (small, non-intrusive):
> "Drink responsibly. Know your limits. 🕐"

---

## User Safety Features

### Opt-Outs
- Users can disable specific prediction categories
- "Don't show cry-in-bathroom risk" (some users find it triggering)

### "Get Home Safe" Button
- Always-visible button on prediction screen
- One tap → Shows local ride services (Uber/Lyft links)
- Never behind a paywall

### Age Gating
- Age verification on first use (self-declared, 18+ / 21+ by region)
- Not a legal requirement for a non-alcohol app, but good practice

---

## Content Moderation

### Image Upload
- Reject NSFW content
- Reject images of minors in nightlife settings
- No storage of face recognition data
- Images auto-deleted after 30 days

### User-Generated Content
- No public feed of predictions (prevents exposure of identifiable photos)
- Sharing is always user-initiated
- Group predictions require all subjects to be adults

## Legal Considerations

| Area           | Action                                              |
|----------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| GDPR           | Data export, deletion, consent, minimal collection   |
| CCPA           | California privacy rights, opt-out of sale           |
| App Store      | "Entertainment" category, no "Food & Drink"          |
| Terms of Use   | Clearly state: entertainment only, not advice        |
| Liability      | Disclaimer: predictions are humor, not behavioral guidance |
| Alcohol laws   | Not serving, selling, or promoting alcohol           |

---

## Crisis Response

### If the app is accused of promoting drinking:
1. Immediate public statement emphasizing entertainment framing
2. Point to safety features (Get Home Safe, responsible messaging)
3. Add additional safety prompts if needed
4. Consult with responsible drinking organizations

### If a user reports harm:
1. Take seriously and respond immediately
2. Offer to delete all their data
3. Provide links to support resources
4. Review and adjust content if pattern emerges
