Ever wonder how we know you're lying when you say "just one more"? Our AI uses a multi-stage pipeline to analyze your nightlife photos:

## Stage 1: Scene Analysis

Our vision model scans your photo for key nightlife signals — drink count, crowd density, lighting conditions, and facial expressions. Each factor contributes to the overall "chaos rating."

## Stage 2: Behavioral Prediction

Using the scene data, our language model predicts your exit time based on patterns from thousands of analyzed nights out. It considers factors like:

- **Time of upload** — posting at 11 PM vs 2 AM tells very different stories
- **Chaos indicators** — more drinks visible = later departure
- **Group dynamics** — solo shots vs group photos have different patterns
- **Environmental cues** — club lighting vs bar ambiance

## Stage 3: The Roast

Because what's a prediction without a little humor? Our AI generates a personalized roast based on your specific scenario. It's brutally honest, but always in good fun.
