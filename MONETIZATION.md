# 💰 Overstay — Monetization Strategy

## Revenue Model: Freemium + Venue B2B

### Free Tier (Core)

All users get:
- 3 predictions per day
- Standard prediction card
- Basic sharing (screenshot / download)
- Beat the AI mode
- Prediction history (last 7 days)

### Premium Tier — "No Curfew" ($2.99/month or $19.99/year)

Premium users get:
- **Unlimited predictions**
- **Premium card designs** (animated, holographic, neon themes)
- **Extended stats** (weekly/monthly chaos reports)
- **Custom roast intensity** (mild → nuclear)
- **Group predictions** (tag friends, individual predictions per person)
- **Morning-after comparison cards**
- **Ad-free experience**
- **Early access to new features**
- **Premium badges**

### One-Time Purchases

| Item                    | Price  | Description                           |
|-------------------------|--------|---------------------------------------|
| Card Theme Pack         | $0.99  | 5 premium card designs                |
| Roast Pack              | $0.99  | Unlock "nuclear" roast mode           |
| Custom Watermark Remove | $1.99  | Remove "overstay.app" from cards      |
| Prediction Boost        | $0.49  | 5 extra predictions (day pass)        |

---

## Venue B2B (Phase 3)

### Venue Dashboard ($49/month)
- See aggregate chaos scores for their venue
- "Tonight's Predicted Chaos" widget for social media
- Branded prediction cards with venue logo
- Customer engagement insights
- "Most Chaotic Night" marketing material

### Venue Promotion ($99/month)
- Featured venue in app
- "Trending Venues" placement
- Custom prediction prompts mentioning the venue
- Event-specific predictions (NYE, Halloween, etc.)

---

## Advertising (Non-Intrusive)

- **Sponsored card backgrounds:** Brands sponsor card themes (subtle)
- **Post-prediction recommendations:** "Need a ride? [Uber/Lyft]"
- **Morning-after suggestions:** "Feeling rough? [hydration brand]"
- **Never interrupt the core prediction flow**

## Key Pricing Decisions

1. **Free tier must feel complete** — users must enjoy the product without paying
2. **Premium is about vanity & social** — cooler cards, more stats, flex features
3. **Never paywall the core prediction** — that kills virality
4. **Venue revenue is the real business** — scalable, B2B, recurring
