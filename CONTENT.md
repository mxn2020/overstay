## AI Prediction Lines

These are the "You said X — That was a lie" style lines the AI generates.

### Exit Time Roasts

```
"You said 'last one' at {time}. That was a lie."
"You told your Uber driver 'be there in 10.' That was {hours} ago."
"Your bed misses you. It'll keep waiting."
"You googled 'bars near me' {n} times tonight."
"At this rate, your alarm clock is just a suggestion."
"You'll check your bank account tomorrow and feel things."
```
